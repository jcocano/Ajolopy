# Expenses

Tlaltipac reimburses the costs that make remote work effective. The
policy is short on purpose: trust the team, audit afterwards.

## What is covered

The company reimburses home office equipment up to a thousand dollars
per year, a co-working stipend of two hundred dollars per month, and
conference attendance up to two thousand dollars per year. Software
subscriptions used for work are covered with no cap.

Travel for team off-sites is booked through the company travel agent
and does not count against any individual limit.

## Filing an expense

Upload the receipt to the company expense tool within thirty days of
the purchase. The tool routes the receipt to the employee's manager
for approval; once approved, the reimbursement is paid in the next
payroll cycle.

Expenses filed more than ninety days after the purchase date are not
reimbursable; this is a hard accounting deadline rather than a policy
choice.

## Receipts

A receipt is required for every line item, even small ones. The
finance team prefers a clear photo over a forwarded email when there
is a choice — the photo carries the date, the merchant, and the total
all in one frame.

If a vendor refuses to issue a receipt, attach a screenshot of the
card statement instead and add a short note to the expense submission.

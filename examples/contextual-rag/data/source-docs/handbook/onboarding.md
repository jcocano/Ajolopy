# Onboarding

Your first two weeks at Tlaltipac. The goal is to ship a small change
to production by the end of week two — not to read every internal doc.

## Day one

Your manager sends you a Linear issue titled "Onboarding: <your name>"
on the morning of your start date. Work through the checklist top to
bottom; it links to every other section of this handbook in the right
order.

The day-one checklist covers laptop pickup, single sign-on
enrolment, joining the engineering Slack channel, and pairing with
your onboarding buddy for a one-hour walkthrough of the codebase.

## Week one

Week one is paired programming. You sit in a daily pairing slot with a
different engineer each day so that by Friday you have touched every
major service. There is no expectation that you push code on your own.

Read `handbook/dev-environment.md` before your first pairing slot so
that your laptop is ready. If anything in that doc is out of date,
file a pull request against this repository as your first contribution.

## Week two

Week two you pick up a `good-first-issue` from Linear and ship it to
production end-to-end: write the change, open the pull request, get it
reviewed under the rules in `handbook/code-review.md`, and walk it
through the release process described in `handbook/deployment.md`.

By Friday of week two you should have one commit in production and one
incident response drill behind you. Your manager schedules a short
retrospective the following Monday.

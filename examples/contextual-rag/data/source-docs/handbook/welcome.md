# Welcome to Tlaltipac

Tlaltipac is a fictional 40-person Series A SaaS company used as the
sample knowledge base for this Ajolopy example. Everything in this
handbook is invented and exists only so the agent has something
concrete to retrieve and cite.

## Who we are

Tlaltipac builds observability tooling for distributed agent runtimes.
The company was founded in 2025 and raised a 12 million dollar Series
A in early 2026. Most of the team works remotely; the legal seat is in
Mexico City and there is a small hub in Berlin.

## What this handbook covers

The handbook is the source of truth for how Tlaltipac operates. It is
split into short topical files so that contextual chunking has clean
boundaries to work with:

- `handbook/welcome.md` — this page.
- `handbook/onboarding.md` — first week for new engineers.
- `handbook/dev-environment.md` — local development setup.
- `handbook/code-review.md` — pull request etiquette.
- `handbook/security.md` — secrets handling and incident response.
- `handbook/deployment.md` — how releases reach production.
- `handbook/time-off.md` — paid time off and holidays.
- `handbook/expenses.md` — expense reimbursement.

## How to read this handbook

Every file is structured as a series of H2 sections so the retriever
can chunk along section boundaries. If you are adding a page, follow
the same convention: a single H1 title, then short H2 sections of one
to four paragraphs each.
